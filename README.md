# Xperise Spend Ops

He thong de xuat chi va phe duyet cho MLX — prototype tuong tac, chay hoan toan tren trinh duyet.

## Chay

Mo `index.html` trong trinh duyet. Khong can server, khong can cai dat.

Ban publish tren Claude: https://claude.ai/artifact/FRbLdN6yp6LCnKRi6VRETP

## Tinh nang

- De xuat chi: bang tien va thue nhieu dong, upload hoa don XML tu dien, hinh thuc thanh toan (CK/Visa/tien mat)
- Cau hinh phe duyet: quy dinh theo phong ban hoac nguoi de xuat, moc tien, cap duyet
- Chuoi duyet: 1-3 cap, chong tu duyet, nguoi du phong, uy quyen duyet
- Dot chi: ke toan lap dot va hen ngay thanh toan, duyet dot, chi tien va dinh kem UNC hang loat
- Xuat file: Excel ngan hang (in hoa khong dau), tai UNC hang loat (.zip)
- Lich su va bao cao tong hop
- Quan ly nguoi dung, NCC, hang muc chi phi
- Dark mode, mobile responsive, Xperise design system

## Du lieu

Prototype luu trong localStorage trinh duyet. Bam "Khoi phuc du lieu mau" o footer de reset.
